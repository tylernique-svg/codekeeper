Privacy notice

This static catalog is designed so we do not collect personal information in the MVP.

  • There are no user accounts, comments, email alerts, or logins.
  • Search runs entirely in your browser against a downloaded JSON file.
  • We do not embed third-party analytics, advertising, or social pixels on these pages.
  • We do not ask children for a name, email, or other personal information.

If a later version adds parent-facing email alerts or accounts, that version will need a reviewed privacy policy and a COPPA-compliant consent flow before collecting personal information from children under 13. See 16 CFR Part 312.

Outbound links (official redeem pages and store pages) are on other companies’ sites and follow those sites’ policies. CheapShark redirect links, when used, may be tracking links as described on the catalog page.

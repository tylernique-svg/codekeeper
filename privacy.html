Privacy notice

Codekeeper is a static catalog of official, publicly shareable game codes, how-to-redeem guidance, and official store prices. It is designed so that we do not ask children for personal information, and so that the pages themselves do not contain accounts, comments, advertising, or tracking scripts.

  • There are no user accounts, comments, chat, email alerts, or logins.
  • The only input on the catalog is a search box. Search runs entirely in your browser against a catalog file already loaded with the page. Search terms are not sent to us or to any other company.
  • These pages do not use cookies or browser storage to identify you, and they do not embed third-party analytics, advertising, social pixels, or font/content delivery networks.
  • We do not ask children for a name, email, phone number, address, or other personal information.

When this catalog is hosted on the public internet (for example, GitHub Pages), the company that serves the files may automatically log technical data such as Internet Protocol (IP) addresses in order to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. Under the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Rule, an IP address can be a “persistent identifier,” which is a form of personal information. See 16 CFR § 312.2 (official eCFR text; you will leave Codekeeper). We do not use host logs to contact anyone, to build a profile, or for advertising, and we will not turn on extra host or traffic-analytics features for this site. If a later version adds email alerts, accounts, or any other collection of personal information from children under 13, that version will need a reviewed privacy policy and a COPPA-compliant parental-consent flow before that collection begins. See 16 CFR Part 312.

Links on the catalog go only to official redeem pages, official publisher or store pages, official rating sources, and the official source used to verify an entry. They open in a new tab. Those other sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control. This snapshot does not include deal-tracker or affiliate redirect links, and those will not be added without a new privacy review.

Contact

Parents and guardians can contact Codekeeper about this notice by email. A dedicated contact email — not a personal telephone number — will be listed here before this catalog is made public. Until that address is added, this site should stay private.
